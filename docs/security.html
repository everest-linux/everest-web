1 - Introduction

Everest Linux is designed with security in mind. This page is meant to familiarize users with its security controls, so that they are both more educated and that they are aware of potential pitfalls that come with the use of Everest.

2 - Glacier

At the heart of Everest Linux is the Glacier package manager. Glacier utilizes the same controls that most other package managers would, including signature verification.

On top of this, Glacier operates on a binary-scope system, USR and SYS.
